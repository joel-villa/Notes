##  John Snow and the Broad St. Pump

- First successful example of data mining
- John Snow + cholera: waterborne disease
  - Narrowed outbreak to a water pump
- People is a big component in data mining: hence why Gallelaeo mapping 
  planetary motion is not considered first example of successful data mining
- Why all these formats csv, hdf, etc? 
  - Out of necessity
- What did they track?
  - deaths_r: number of deaths per house
  - dis_pestf: distance from a pest field
  - dis_sewers: distance from a sewer outlet
  - dis_bspump: The actual source of the disease
  - etc.
- How to convince the state to seal the pump?
  - Must statistically prove that all other hypothesis are less likely than the
  hypothesis that it is the water pump.
- Hypothetical Job:
	- What if your boss comes to you with a bunch of data and says: 'tell me
	what's in there?'
