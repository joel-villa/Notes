## Definitions

Ad Hoc Network: "A wireless network that allows easy connection establishment between wireless client devices in the same physical area without the use of an infrastructure device, such as an access point or a base station." [[https://csrc.nist.gov/glossary/term/ad_hoc_network|link]]
