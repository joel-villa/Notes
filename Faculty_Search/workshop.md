# Faculty Workshop Notes

## Conflict of Interests

- Have a conversation about it

## Maintain Confidentiality

- Names of applicants must be kept confidential: do not reveal info about who 
  applied
- Do not discuss candidates over email or text
- Matrix: evaluation of all the candidates (can be sent over email)
- Let the candidates know when their interview information goes outside of the 
  commitee
- Can communicate over Zoom 'be careful'

## PAM

- PAM needs to be approved, before application published
- Minimum Qualifications:
    - No to any -> do not review further
- Preferred Qualifications:
    - Expertise that is key to position
    - How badly do we want to have an interview?
    - How do we evaluate the preferred qualifications? How do we ask an 
      applicant to demonstrate strength within a preferreed qualification
    - Required preferred qualification: Serve local & global communities 
      (replace DEI)
        - Cannot ask for a statement that addresses a particular preferred 
          qualification, can for multiple
- Requested Materials:
    - In a letter, address all of the prefferred qualifications
    - Only materials that you ask for will be reviewed
    - Letters of Reference or Referrence checks?
        - Cons of Letters of Reference: letter written by AI, candidate writes 
          letter, letters tend to be exclusively positive,
        - Phone Calls with references can be forthcoming:
            - Do not do off-list reference checks
            - Ask for references at time of in-person interview: assurance that 
            - Reference calls: do not record
            - Same list of questions per reference calls
- Search and Recruit:
    - Ask grad students where are good places to advertise?
- Matrix (rubric):
    - Minimum Evidence of support vs. Maximum Evidence of support

## Evaluating Candidates

- Stick to script: only evaluate on what you said you'd say you'd evaluate on
- Committee can access links on CV
- If it's common in field, can ask for Google Scholar page

## Second Look

- From minimum qualifications to long-short list
- Drawing the line, make sure it is in the right place:
    - Should those right below the line be there?

## Interviews

- Same time limit per candidate
- What happens when you don't have a chance to ask a question

## Who to Bring to Campus?

- Who best represents those prefferred criteria?

## What About..

- Extending best consideration date -> have to review all applicants
- What if you decided you want to switch something about the process afterward?
    - Can do that, but must document it (final justification reflects those 
      changes)
- International Candidates
    - $100k fee for new VISA applicants, not fully litigated:
        - If applicants already have some sort of VISA status, 100k fee doesn't 
          apply
        - Traditional historic rates: $10k, does the department have funding? 
- What ensurances can the college provide? Reach out to them!!
- Don't like in-person candidates? Can go back to the pool

## Campus Visits

- Require Dean's Approval
- Search Committee organizes agenda, and keeps control of it
- Contact Info
- Schedule Interview Breaks
- What would I want if I were this person?
- They're Interviewing you:
    - Could I see myself in Albuquerque? 
    - Meet with Students, Faculty, Staff, Stakeholders, check audio/video 
      equipment in advance
- What can we ask, what can we not ask?
    - Ask anything job relevant
    - Avoid anything not job relevant:
        - How many children do you have? Are they married? Cannot ask about 
          VISA status or citizenship status 
        - Can talk about it if they bring it up: "what job opportunities for 
          my partner here?"         
- Campus Visit Evaluation Form: have department review the candidates as a 
  group
    - Ask for feedback along the way, so people remember
    - Do not ask whole community to rank candidates, but you can ask them to 
      give feedback
    - Internal candidate: don't show up during the interview of the other 
      candidates
- Can We Rank Candidates?

## Final Processes

- Hiring Justification Memo:
    - Updates PAM
    - History of Search process
    - Names and schedule of all the finalists
    - Strengths of the proposed hire, and everyone else
    - Please don't give information as to why someone is not prefferred
    - Composite Matrix (averages the community member's matrix scores)
    - Should be clear that everyone was treated similarly
- Offer and Negotiation:
    - Hiring officer + Dean
- What to gie to who:
    - 5 year dept keeping:
        - spreadsheets, matrices, try to keep objective (may be read by others)
        - Evaluation forms from candidate visits
        - Composite Matrix
        - Notes: 
            - Want to keep notes very very careful (can be potentially viewed)
            - Should be related to preferred qualifications (that could be a
              problem)
    - Info for school & college
        - Composite Matrix
    - OAP:
        - Candidate's CV
        - Candidate's official transcripts
        - Compinsation ANalysis
        - Draft Letter of Offer
- Official Transcripts are a slow down:
    - If at the hire we don't have official, it can take 10 days, can be 
      biggest delay in getting the letter of offer issued
        - UNM uses old version of Official transcript
        - Highest level transcript
    - Could ask all those that you fly in to bring transcripts
